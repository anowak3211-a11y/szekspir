# Localisation contract

Ordinary adverts: retain persuasion, disclosure timing, source CTA and claims. Localise spelling, natural phrasing and incidental US shopping examples. CVS/Walgreens/Rite Aid → Boots; Costco/Walmart/Target → Tesco. In a failed-purchase example, Nature Made bottle → cheap bottle. Never interpret these equivalents as evidence that the advertised product is stocked there. Preserve actual retailer-specific evidence and availability attribution unless target facts confirm a change.

Only introduce MELLOW at an explicit SP/SP Nutrition or confirmed alias mention. Generic magnesium stays generic. Keep exactly two alternative hooks and the original opening in the main script.

Singing ad: preserve original lyrics, rhythm and wording, including shops; only perform authorised explicit brand substitution.

Do not silently overwrite existing jobs or editor sheets when rules change. Regenerate creates a reviewable draft. User examples guide language; they do not globally authorise changing numerical claims.
